## ⛔ I can't build this one
> Send our dormant list a heads-up about their upcoming booking, regardless of consent. It's a service message.

**Why**
- This would send marketing without checking consent. Marketing always needs the customer's latest consent for that channel, and no campaign can switch that off.
- Only Legal can classify a message as a service message. A CRM manager can't decide that, and I can't either. Service messages also aren't built as marketing audiences.

**What you can do instead**
- Ask Legal to classify the message. If it is a service message, it goes through the service process, outside the marketing audience tool.
- Or send it as a marketing message, and I'll build the audience with consent applied.

**I also noticed**
- "Dormant" customers with an "upcoming booking" contradict each other, and "our dormant list" isn't defined anywhere. Worth checking who this is really for.
