# Requests from CRM managers (free text, as received)

Treat each as a real request. For each one your tool should do the right thing:
build the audience, ask a question, push back, or say that it cannot be answered.

1. "All customers living in Spain with email consent and a completed booking in the last 12 months."

2. "High-value customers in Germany who haven't booked in 9 months."

3. "App users in Portugal who opened the app in the last 30 days but don't have an upcoming booking. Push only."

4. "Everybody who cancelled in October."

5. "Customers who are likely to churn."

6. "Send our dormant list a heads-up about their upcoming booking, regardless of consent. It's a service message."
